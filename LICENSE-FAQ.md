# LICENSE FAQ

This document explains Sydeso licensing in plain language.

## What license does this repository use?

This repository uses Business Source License 1.1 (`BUSL-1.1`).

The exact terms are in `LICENSE`.

## Can I self-host Sydeso?

Yes.

The Additional Use Grant allows production use, including self-hosting for internal or personal use.

## What is restricted before the Change Date?

You cannot offer the Licensed Work to third parties on a hosted, managed, or embedded basis in a way that competes with Sydeso commercial offerings.

See the `LICENSE` for exact wording.

## What happens on the Change Date?

On June 1, 2030, the Change License applies:

- GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPLv3)

## Is this repository open source today?

It is source-available under BSL 1.1 before the Change Date.

After the Change Date, it transitions to AGPLv3 under the terms in `LICENSE`.

## Do contributors assign copyright?

No assignment is required by default in this repository.

By contributing, you agree your contribution is licensed under the repository license.

Use the process described in `COMMERCIAL.md`.
