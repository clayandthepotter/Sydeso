# Code of Conduct

Sydeso follows a contributor covenant-style standard of respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing issues directly.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect differing experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive or bad-faith participation.
- Abusive language or intimidation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.

To report an issue, use the security/contact process in `SECURITY.md` or contact the maintainers privately.
